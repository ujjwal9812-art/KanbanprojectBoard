How to use
  • Drag cards between columns. Drag the grip on a column header to reorder columns.
  • Click a card to edit its due date, priority, description and members.
  • Keyboard: focus a card, then press Enter to edit or Alt + Left/Right arrow to move it between columns.
  • Use Export to download a backup, Import to restore one, and Reset to go back to the demo data.
  • Click a column title to rename it. Everything saves automatically in this browser.
